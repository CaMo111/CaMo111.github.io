Tutoring

Working for tutoring businesses, I have seen first hand the flaws that have been subjected upon students & parents paying good money to improve their childrens education. Underpaying is ubiqutous, with most companies pocketing at least 50% of the profit with esentially no impact in the students progression. Undertraining is common, schedules are rigid and the experience is standardized.

I offer a more personalised experience for my clients compared to company-based tutoring. By working one-on-one with a student I tailor my teaching methods to fit their needs, learning styles and pace. This approach results in more immediate feedback, focused attention and thereby better comprehension of subject material.

I Provide Three Key Tutoring Services

English

Achieving a 45 Study Score in English, I have come to the understanding that teaching literature is more about the comprehension of ideas to that of pure writing ability. By tutoring English with me, I will demonstrate how to scrutinize elements of text, persuasion and literature techniques or even speech and communication skills.

Maths

Mathematical tuition can range anywhere from basic linear equations, to advanced computational linear algebra containing abstract vector spaces. Irrespective of the difficulty, I will direct clients to an understanding that by breaking down the abstract nature of a topic, we can find real applications and come to a deeper comprehension of mathematical concepts.

Programming

I offer tutoring for programming, but my beginner python package available gives a good multi-day introduction into how to write scripts in python. This is useful regardless of your age and mathematical experience. It starts off simple but by the end will challenge you to think about how and what must be done to solve a problem using programming.
